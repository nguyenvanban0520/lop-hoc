## Prompt

Use case: identity-preserve compositing. Create ONE game sprite-sheet asset for a Vietnamese classroom game. Reference image is the user's own face, identity reference only. Preserve his REAL PHOTOGRAPHIC face, recognizable exact facial proportions, short buzz haircut, black thick rectangular glasses, friendly smile. Do NOT turn his face into an anime face or an illustration. Improve exposure gently while preserving likeness. Composite that photoreal head onto a cute illustrated MALE chibi teacher body: light blue long-sleeved collared men's shirt tucked into navy trousers, dark belt, brown male dress shoes. Fully clothed professional male teacher. Large head, small chibi body. One transparent PNG sprite sheet in a precise 2 by 2 equal grid. Each cell contains ONE whole full-body man centered, feet aligned at same relative height, same head size, same shirt/trousers, head fully within cell. Cell upper left: standing relaxed hands down. Upper right: one hand pointing to viewer's right to teach. Lower left: waving hello. Lower right: thumbs up encouraging pupils. Same face, same frontal camera and scale in all 4. Keep all limbs within their own cell with generous transparent gutters; no crossing cell borders. Canvas aspect ratio 2:3, each cell aspect 2:3 so male body can fit upright. No background, no grid lines, no lettering, no labels, no floor shadow, no scenery. Real face on illustrated chibi body is intentional. No female body, no dress, no ao dai.
